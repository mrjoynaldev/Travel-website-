# 🌿 SUNDARBAN HILSA FESTIVAL 🌿

### A Celebration of Bengali Flavours, Monsoon & Hilsa

**Taste • Tradition • Travel — The Sundarbans**

Good news for food lovers and travel enthusiasts!

Following the wonderful response of previous years, **Sundarban Yatri** proudly presents the **Sundarban Hilsa Festival** — a special monsoon experience celebrating Bengal's most beloved fish, the Hilsa (Ilish).

From **July to September**, experience the magic of the monsoon in the Sundarbans, combined with an indulgent spread of traditional Bengali delicacies.

---

## The Hilsa Experience

Enjoy a delicious variety of authentic Hilsa preparations, including:

- Mustard Hilsa
- Steamed Hilsa
- Hilsa with Pui Greens
- Doi Hilsa
- Hilsa Biryani
- And more seasonal Bengali specialities

Along with the culinary experience, enjoy a memorable journey through the Sundarbans with day-long boat and launch cruising, comfortable overnight accommodation, and traditional cultural entertainment including Adivasi folk performances and Baul music.

# ITINERARY

## Day 1

- Journey starts from **Canning / Sonakhali** and proceeds to **Gosaba** by boat.
- Visit **Rabindranath Tagore's Bungalow** and **Hamilton Bungalow**.
- After lunch, arrival at **Pakhiralay / Bali Island / Dayapur / Jharkhali** and hotel check-in.
- Evening leisure by the riverside or explore the surrounding nature.
- Overnight stay at the hotel.

## Day 2

- Jungle safari begins from **Sajnekhali Watch Tower**.
- Visit **Sudhanyakhali**, **Dobanki Canopy Walk**, **Pirkhali**, **Gazikhali**, **Bonbibi Bharani**, **Deul Bharani**, and **Panchamukhani**.
- Visit **Jharkhali Tiger Rescue Centre**.
- Full-day cruise through rivers, creeks, and mangrove forest.
- Overnight stay at the hotel.

## Day 3

- Morning visit to **Loknath Baba Temple** and **Bonmata Temple**.
- Explore local villages and nearby sightseeing points.
- Return to **Sonakhali Ferry Ghat** to conclude the tour.
