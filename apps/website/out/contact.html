Where we operate

Find us at Sonakhali, Sundarban, West Bengal, India. Tours run ex-Kolkata / Canning / Sonakhali — pickup, drive, licensed boat, guide, stay and meals arranged from there. There is no walk-in office; the delta is our workplace.

Mon–Sat, 9am–7pm IST. Messages outside hours are answered the next working morning.

Before you write

Most travellers come via Kolkata to Godkhali / Canning (2.5–4 hrs by road + boat). Share your starting point and we will map the smoothest route — train, car and boat handover included.
