# Sundarban Yatri — Lead Engine Roadmap

**Goal:** turn trip planners into calls, WhatsApp chats and `/hire`
enquiries — then into booked seats. Content + proof + distribution serve that
one number: **enquiries per week**. Skills: `LEAD-SKILL.md` (funnel) ·
`TOUR-SKILL.md` (products) · `SHOWCASE-SKILL.md` (proof) · `SOCIAL-SKILL.md`.

## 0) Reality check

- A Sundarban enquiry is worth far more than ad clicks: one 2D/1N family
  booking beats a year of AdSense on a new site. **No ads until 50k+ monthly
  visits** — and maybe never; seats scale better than banners.
- Current assets: site ✔ · 4 tour products ✔ · Studio CMS ✔ · video reviews +
  food menu engines ✔ · MCP agent control ✔ · Instagram automation ✔.
- Missing (in order): proof stock (reviews/dishes) → seasonal guides →
  distribution rhythm → lead-speed discipline.

## 1) Positioning (locked)

*Your trusted guide to planning a Sundarban journey.* Not the cheapest, not
luxury — the operator travellers trust with a remote forest trip. Proof
(real clips, real plates, honest odds) over promises.

## 2) The machine (how money happens)

```
Guides answer trip questions (SEO + AI citations)
  → Tour pages convert interest into intent
    → Call/WhatsApp closes (same-day reply wins)
      → /hire catches international + after-hours
        → Studio pipeline: new → contacted → won/lost
          → Lost leads recycled every festival season
```

Every new feature must shorten this path or feed it proof. Anything else waits.

## 3) 90-day plan

### Phase 1 — Proof stock (days 1–30). Goal: 6 reviews, 8 dishes, pipeline habit.
- [ ] Crew routine: ask every happy group for a 20-sec clip (SHOWCASE §1).
- [ ] Photograph every real spread; publish 8 dishes with honest captions.
- [ ] Re-verify all 4 tours (timings, stay, price notes) for the season.
- [ ] Lead discipline: same-day first reply, statuses moved daily.
- [ ] Publish 4 cornerstone guides: reach, cost, best-time, 2D/1N itinerary.
- **KPI:** 5+ enquiries/week, median first-reply < 24h.

### Phase 2 — Seasonal capture (days 31–60). Goal: rank for peak planners.
- [ ] Cost + itinerary + safari-odds + packing guides (one cluster at a time).
- [ ] Instagram/FB rhythm from the proof loop (SOCIAL §2); 1 reel/week minimum.
- [ ] Comment→DM automation on the best-performing reel.
- [ ] Answer 3 trip questions/week (Reddit/Quora), value-first.
- **KPI:** 10+ enquiries/week, 1+ reel driving DMs, guides ranking for 3+ trip queries.

### Phase 3 — Convert + compound (days 61–90). Goal: bookings, recycling.
- [ ] Festival/weekend push: WhatsApp broadcast to old lost/contacted leads.
- [ ] Double down on the top-2 converting guides (update, expand, interlink).
- [ ] Collect post-trip clips on every departure (compounding asset).
- [ ] Review win-rate by tour; fix the weakest tour page, not the ads.
- **KPI:** first `won` bookings tracked end-to-end, win rate ≥ 20%, proof wall growing monthly.

## 4) Weekly operating cadence (owner: 4–5 hrs)

- Mon: pipeline review (new→contacted, lost reasons) — 30 min
- Tue: 1 guide or tour-page improvement — 2 hrs
- Thu: reel + FB post + 3 forum answers — 1 hr
- Sun: metrics (enquiries, sources, win rate, proof added) — 30 min

## 5) What NOT to do

- No price wars, fake urgency, or invented sightings — ever.
- No new tour products until Custom enquiries overflow.
- No ad spend before the funnel converts organic enquiries.
- No platform sprawl: Instagram + Facebook + WhatsApp first; rest later.
- No publishing without the POST-WRITING-SKILL QA gate.

## 6) Metrics that matter (Studio weekly)

Enquiries/week · source page per lead · median new→contacted hours ·
won/(won+lost) · reviews + dishes published · CTA clicks on top pages.
Traffic without enquiries is a hobby — fix the commercial path first.
