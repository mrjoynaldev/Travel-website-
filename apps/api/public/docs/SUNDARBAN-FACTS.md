## 1. Where this is

The Sundarbans: the world's largest mangrove forest, spread across West
Bengal (India) and Bangladesh. **We sell the Indian side**, entered from the
South 24 Parganas side of West Bengal. Nearest big city: **Kolkata**.

## 2. Gateways & routes (how travellers actually arrive)

- **Kolkata → Godkhali** by road (the standard start for boat tours; most
  itineraries meet around Canning/Godkhali belt and board there).
- **Canning** is the railhead travellers use from Kolkata (local train),
  then road to the jetty.
- From the jetty, everything moves by **motor boat/launch** — creeks and
  rivers are the roads here. There are no self-drive safaris; movement inside
  forest beats follows designated routes with permissions.
- International travellers typically route via Kolkata (flight) + 1 night
  buffer before/after the Sundarban leg — say this explicitly in guides.

## 3. Places to name correctly

| Place | What it is |
|---|---|
| Sajnekhali | Watchtower + interpretation centre; classic first safari stop |
| Dobanki (Dobanki) | Canopy walk — the elevated walkway experience |
| Jharkhali | Butterfly park, rescue/interpretation side; deeper itineraries |
| Gosaba | Historic Hamilton estate area, village + market halt |
| Pakhiralay / Dayapur belt | Riverside stay villages |
| Godkhali | The main boarding jetty for tours |

Use full names on first mention. Never invent a watchtower, island or
"secret spot".

## 4. Wildlife honesty block (copy-safe wording)

> The Bengal tiger lives here and sightings happen — but tigers are wild,
> elusive, and never guaranteed. What travellers routinely see: spotted deer,
> wild boar, estuarine crocodiles, monitor lizards, and rich birdlife
> (kingfishers, egrets, raptors; winter brings migrants). Wildlife sightings
> depend on tide, season, silence and luck.

Any deviation from this block needs a sourced reason.

## 5. Seasons (read with LEAD-SKILL §3)

- **Oct–Mar:** main season. Nov–Feb adds winter migrants; Dec–Jan weekends
  are the yearly demand peak.
- **Apr–May:** hot and thin crowds.
- **Jun–Sep:** monsoon; heavy rain, limited/paused operations in peak weeks.
- Festival pulses (winter fairs, hilsa season) move demand year to year —
  confirm dates with the owner before publishing.

## 6. Verify-every-season list (never carry over blindly)

Forest entry rules and fees · boat timings and sharing rules · stay partners
and room categories · price notes on all tours · festival dates · which tours
run in monsoon · phone/WhatsApp/email on the contact page · meeting points.

## 7. Language rules

- "Safari" here = boat-based forest beats + watchtowers, not jeeps. Never
  write jeep-safari copy.
- Distances in km, times in IST, food terms travellers know (veg/non-veg,
  "meals on the boat").
- Kolkata, not Calcutta, in new content. IST times like "8:10 AM".
